Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks normally associated with human thinking, such as recognising images, understanding language, or making decisions.

AI is a broad umbrella term. The four cards below cover some of its most important subfields, each linked to a trusted resource for further reading.

Machine Learning

Systems that learn patterns from data instead of following hand-written rules.
