What is Computer Science?

At its core, computer science is the study of algorithms: precise, step-by-step instructions for solving a problem. A recipe for a cake and a sorting algorithm both describe a sequence of steps, but computer science asks how to make those steps efficient, correct, and reusable.

The field covers many areas, including data structures, operating systems, networks, databases, and theory of computation. The Harvard CS50 course is a well-known starting point for beginners.

A brief history

Early computers like the ENIAC filled entire rooms and were programmed by physically rewiring circuits. Over the decades, computers became smaller, faster, and far easier to program, leading to the personal computers and smartphones we use today.

For a deeper historical overview, the Computer History Museum has an extensive archive.
