The Future of Technology

Computer science keeps opening new frontiers. Quantum computing aims to solve certain problems far faster than today's machines by using the principles of quantum mechanics instead of classical bits.

Cloud computing has already changed how software is built and delivered, letting anyone rent computing power instead of owning physical servers.
Learn more from the IBM Quantum overview.

Robotics and smart cities

Robotics combines computer science with mechanical engineering to build machines that can sense and act in the physical world, from factory arms to home vacuum robots.

Meanwhile, smart cities use networks of sensors and software to manage traffic, energy, and public services more efficiently. The field is still young, and today's CSE students will help shape what comes next.
