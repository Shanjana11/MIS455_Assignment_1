Introduction to Computer Science

Computer science is the study of computers and the things we can do with them: storing information, running calculations, and automating tasks. It combines mathematics, logic, and engineering to solve problems with code.

This site is a short tour for anyone starting out. Read about what CS actually covers, compare programming languages, and explore where artificial intelligence fits in.

Why it matters

Nearly every industry today, from medicine to agriculture, relies on software. Learning computer science gives you a way to build tools instead of just using them, and to understand the systems that already run daily life.

Quick fact: the first programmable computers appeared in the 1940s, but the ideas behind algorithms go back centuries earlier.
